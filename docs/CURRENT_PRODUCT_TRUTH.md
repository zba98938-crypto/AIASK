> **Interpretation.** Incubation is **not idle-spinning**: 12 real
> promotions occurred (3 distinct strategies, last on 2026-10-19). It is
> also not the quiet success path most operators would assume — 98% of gate
> reports fail, overwhelmingly on missing statistical evidence rather than
> weak metrics, which is the correct fail-closed behaviour for strategies
> submitted without a measured evidence chain. `is_formal_ready` is **not a
> stored column** anywhere in the running DB; promotion is recorded as
> `decision='promote'` rows in `strategy_incubation_metrics`.
>
> The readiness gate (`scripts/aiask_readiness.py`) now reports two
> explicitly separated verdicts:
>
> - `summary.environment_ready` / `product.backend_ready` — **environment
>   pre-check only** (interpreter, source tree, importability). It is
>   labelled `gate_type: environment_precheck` in the JSON output.
> - `summary.business_verdict` / `business_acceptance` — **business
>   acceptance** (SQLite reachable and non-empty). A green pre-check never
>   implies this second verdict; a fake `AIASK_SQLITE_PATH` yields
>   `environment_ready: true, business_verdict: fail` — which is the point.
>
> The endpoint drift gate now compares the allowlist against the **live**
> FastAPI route set (`reports/code-graph/live-routes.json`, regenerated by
> `scripts/code_graph/export_live_routes.py`), not against two committed
> static files. Stale static `/v1/desktop/*` allowlist entries are reclassified
> as `removed_desktop_catchall`; 44 genuinely new application paths
> (`/v1/finance/*`, `/v1/research/*`, `/v1/users/*`, …) were added to the
> allowlist and are now policed.

### Factory command semantics

| Surface | Execution model | Success proves | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| `factory once` | free-data/strategy one-shot | selected one-shot subprocesses exited successfully | other factories, scheduling, or continuous health |
| `factory cycle` | selected research factories run once in sequence | that single research cycle completed | supervisor health or 24h operation |
| `factory ops-start` | starts the four-runtime process supervisor | supervisor survived startup and owns a managed process group | multi-day soak, evidence quality, or formal promotion |
| `run_strategy_factory_quality_session.py` | explicitly bounded quality session | only the emitted session evidence and duration | general production certification |

The historical filename `run_three_factories.py` supervises four runtimes. It
is retained as a compatibility entrypoint; product-facing commands and status
use the term **four-runtime supervisor**. `factory cycle` and `ops-start` must
never be described as the 24h quality-session path.
